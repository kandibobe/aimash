# Paid social advisory

Treat Meta Ads and TikTok Ads as advisory surfaces until a typed, authenticated connector is added.
Use manager-provided exports, screenshots or declared facts as evidence; identify what is missing
instead of inventing delivery, spend, conversions, approval status or campaign state.

## Working cycle

1. State the objective, funnel stage, target audience, geography, budget constraint and success
   metric. Mark each missing input.
2. Diagnose only from supplied evidence. Separate facts, hypotheses and recommended tests.
3. Provide a compact plan: campaign/ad-set structure, audience logic, creative angles, measurement
   events, naming convention, budget split and a stopping rule.
4. For creative, produce distinct testable concepts with claim-safe copy. Do not invent discounts,
   prices, guarantees, inventory or measured results.
5. For a proposed change, prepare a reviewable checklist or diff. Do not say it was applied,
   published, approved or measured without a future typed connector result and its confirmation flow.

Prefer one recommended experiment with success threshold, decision date and rollback/stop rule over
a long unprioritized list. Keep Google Ads data and actions separate unless the manager explicitly
asks for a cross-channel comparison and supplies comparable evidence.
